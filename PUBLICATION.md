# Fixprio Design Lab

Public prototype and design documentation for Fixprio, a single-shop business ecosystem for electronics commerce and repair.

- **Redfix**: catalog, orders, inventory, point of sale, supplier sourcing and purchasing.
- **Revio**: repair workflows, device identification and parts allocation.
- **Shared capabilities**: customer directory, QR scanning, label printing and simulated WhatsApp notifications.

## Current milestone

V20 covers purchase orders, partial receiving, supplier payment recording and inventory synchronization in the standalone prototype. V20.0 is archive preservation. V20.1 is the proposed QR architecture and risk audit; V20.2–V20.9 are planned extensions for incoming product registration, advance label preparation, scanner UX, serialized units, batches, locations, printing and hardware acceptance.

## Publication boundaries

This repository is public by the owner's choice. Only reviewed, synthetic prototype material belongs here. Never publish credentials, customer or supplier personal information, live business records, provider secrets, or confidential third-party materials. Public visibility does not imply an open-source license or production readiness.

Prototype storage, roles, payment records and provider integrations are simulated. Production authorization, concurrency, persistence, hardware acceptance and live messaging must be independently implemented and verified. Do not deploy the demo against live systems.
