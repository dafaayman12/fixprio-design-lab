# V20.3 — Purchase-order label preparation

Builds on V20.2 and the owner-approved Take App parity principle. No new page: the existing purchase detail has a Print labels action that opens existing Label Studio in purchase context. Ordinary product identities are reused, not newly issued for every physical unit.

- Preselect only outstanding eligible purchase lines with quantity ordered minus received; do not include received lines or unrelated products. Clear any prior label selection. Copies can be reduced, deselected, or restored within outstanding limits. Return to the purchase, or open ordinary Label Studio to clear purchase context.
- Existing maximum 250 labels per print job is retained. Larger orders are not silently truncated: reduce quantities manually and track previously printed copies manually. No physical print confirmation or print-history accounting.
- Preparation rejects cancelled/fully received orders, unsupported products, blocked registry and unauthorized demo roles. Print and SVG export check the current purchase version, permissions, and selected records; stale purchase context must be reopened. Purchase version changes, even payment recording, conservatively invalidate preparation.
- No stock movement, receipt, payment, or purchase status change on label preparation/printing. Printing is not receipt. Individual serialized and variant-specific identities remain out of scope; ordinary repeated labels are identical. Existing purchase and catalog permissions apply.
