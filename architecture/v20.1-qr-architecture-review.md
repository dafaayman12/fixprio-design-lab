## Reproduced findings
Chromium, synthetic page and simulated localStorage Map:
- Generic receiving accepts a serialized item's product QR: add=true, commit=true, stock delta +1. Blocker for unique unit tracking.
- Generic receiving increases stock for a product on an open PO while PO received stays 0. Separate non-PO receipt may be legitimate; require explicit origin.
- Direct StockOperations.execute under Reviewer role succeeds, stock delta +1. Simulated UI gating is not command authorization.
- Same-key purchase receipt replay succeeds once, replays true, stock delta +1 total. Positive but session-only.
- External URL resolves unknown. Positive, not full security validation.
- Label storage contains version, catalog, tokens; serialized and repair maps are not persisted there.

## Recommended sequence and open gates
A then B are blocking foundations; C before pre-arrival label preparation; D/E before hardware acceptance. Identity ownership and tracking policy need owner agreement before interface design. Test permanent IDs across devices, mutable SKU aliases and collisions, revoked identity, explicit stock origin, wrong role, reserved stock, unique serial duplicate prevention, receipt/activation atomicity, response-loss recovery, zero stock effect from print, cancellation, no customer data in QR, and actual 203/300 DPI printer/camera/keyboard scanner workflows. Physical hardware and cross-browser fallback are not verified. Do not assume a common physical database. No production change, no real data, no final design approval. Full archive transfer remains independent.
