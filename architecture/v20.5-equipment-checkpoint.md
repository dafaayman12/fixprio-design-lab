# V20.5 — Individual equipment tracking checkpoint

Extends V20.4 with an opt-in individual-unit workflow, using existing Scanner, Label Studio and POS. No new pages. Ordinary products retain their existing workflow.

- Owner can prepare a permanent unit QR using a manufacturer serial for the existing Rigol demo product or an eligible new zero-stock unpublished product without variants, barcode aliases, purchase lines or cart entries. Serial is normalized to uppercase and unique within a product. Existing identity mappings are preserved; new identities are stored in browser-local registry. Preparing or printing a label does not receive stock.
- Scan individual label in lookup mode to confirm an explicit non-purchase physical receipt. Pending becomes available; stock increases by exactly one. Duplicate and generic receipts rejected. Purchase-order receipt for serialized items remains disabled.
- Available unit can be added to existing POS from scanner lookup or exact-code POS search. Cart records the serial, prevents repeat addition, and groups exact tokens for shared StockOperations validation. Checkout retains serial and unit token in order lines; sold unit cannot be sold again. This is a sale reference, not a warranty entitlement or claim-management system.
- Existing legacy aggregate equipment balances are not automatically allocated to units. Seed serial and restored individual identities are recognized but unavailable for operations because operational state is session-only. Never infer received/sold state from a persisted label. No automated migration of existing equipment.
- Unit receipt/sale role enforcement uses existing simulated stock permissions; no new staff roles. Generic operations and repair allocations remain blocked for tracked products. No extra warehouse workflow.
