## Changes
- A common genericStockEligible predicate blocks known serialized and variant products at StockOperations.execute, scanner batch preparation/add, POS add/checkout, purchase eligibility and lookup stock action UI. Exact-unit operations remain unavailable pending individual tracking. This prevents the reproduced generic serialized receipt bypass, without making serialized stock appear sellable through ordinary POS.
- StockOperations.execute checks simulated role before replay or mutation. Owner can adjust/count/receive/sell, Operator can sell and retain existing repair permissions, Reviewer cannot mutate through these stock commands. Proposed role policy for owner review; production staff capabilities remain to be established.
- Receive requires explicit origin: adjustment or purchase. Included in idempotency fingerprint and movement records. Generic receipt UI explicitly says non-purchase and directs supplier orders to Purchases; purchase receipt calls existing command with purchase origin. This is origin classification, not a proof that every physical delivery was tied to a purchase or that a direct browser caller cannot spoof the origin.
- Purchase receipt requires nonempty idempotency key. No additional screens or dependency. V20 original preserved.

## Remaining
Local browser JavaScript is not security enforcement; records remain synthetic/session-only apart from existing catalog metadata storage. No persistent registry implementation or migration yet, no unit-specific receipt/sale lifecycle, no real cross-device authorization, transaction/recovery, scanner/printer acceptance, or production deployment. The generic adjustment path may still be used intentionally or mistakenly instead of a purchase receipt; enforce domain command provenance and policy server-side during integration. Only known fixture serial mappings drive eligibility today; explicit durable tracking mode required. Do not claim Take App parity. No production repository touched.
